## ASSET 1 — the YouTube thumbnail

### 1. Title to type into Claude Design

```
SHADAVA — Hexatonic Live Thumbnail
```

### 2. Copy-paste prompt

```
Design a YouTube thumbnail, 1280x720 pixels, for a music-theory lesson by Jason Zac
of Nathaniel School of Music. Deliver 3 distinct variations.

THE ONE IDEA THE IMAGE MUST COMMUNICATE INSTANTLY:
A piano keyboard with exactly ONE white key struck out in red — and the number 6
as the biggest thing on screen. The video is about deleting one note from the
seven-note major scale to get a six-note scale. If a viewer understands the image,
they already understand the video.

MANDATORY ELEMENTS:
1. A piano keyboard, shown as one clean octave, viewed straight on or in a slight
   three-quarter angle. Photographic or beautifully illustrated — not a flat
   cartoon, not a MIDI-editor screenshot.
2. Exactly ONE white key crossed out with a bold red X or a red strike-through.
   It must be unmistakable which single key is being removed. That key is the 4th
   (the F key, if the keyboard starts on C). Every other key stays clean and
   untouched — the contrast is the whole point.
3. The numeral "6" as the hero element. Very large, confident, occupying roughly a
   third of the frame's height. It should read at thumbnail size on a phone.
4. Jason Zac's face on the right third of the frame — calm, warm, one eyebrow
   slightly raised, mouth CLOSED. Absolutely no open-mouthed shock face, no
   pointing at the text, no hands on cheeks. He is a serious educator, not a
   reaction thumbnail.
5. The Nathaniel School of Music logo — the real N mark — placed tastefully and
   small, one spot only. Bottom-left or bottom-right. Do not invent a logo; use
   the supplied PNG. White version on dark backgrounds.

COLOUR — this is a COLOURED thumbnail (Nathaniel School of Music uses colour for
normal episodes and black-and-white only for its Free Style series, so colour is
required here). Use the house palette:
   deep red      #8B1E24   (the strike-out X, accents)
   gold          #C9A227   (the numeral 6, highlights)
   cream         #F4EFE4   (light type, keyboard whites)
   near-black    #14110F   (background)
Background should be dark and rich so the gold 6 and the red X both punch.

TYPE:
   Headline / numerals : Archivo (heavy weight)
   Any serif accent    : Cormorant
   Small caps / labels : IBM Plex Mono
Maximum FOUR words of text besides the numeral. Suggested: "NO WRONG NOTES" or
"DELETE ONE NOTE". Keep type off the extreme corners so YouTube's duration badge
and progress bar do not collide with it.

MOOD: premium, confident, slightly scholarly. Think a beautifully designed music
textbook cover, not a hype-driven YouTube thumbnail. Restrained, high contrast,
zero clutter.

DO NOT:
- Do not add staff notation, clefs or note-heads. The keyboard alone carries it.
- Do not use arrows, circles, glow effects or drop shadows on the type.
- Do not write "world's first" or any superlative claim anywhere.
- Do not spell the name as "Jason Zak", "Jason Zach" or "Jack". It is JASON ZAC.
- Do not put a URL on the thumbnail.
- Do not use stock-photo shocked faces or generic music clip art.

DELIVER: 3 variations at 1280x720, plus a 1:1 crop of the strongest one for
Instagram. Export PNG.
```

## ASSET 2 — the app identity (do this one second)

### 1. Title to type into Claude Design

```
SHADAVA — App Identity and Landing Hero
```

### 2. Copy-paste prompt

```
Design the visual identity and landing-page hero for SHADAVA, a web app for
practising six-note (hexatonic) musical scales. Built by Nathaniel School of
Music. Audience: serious music students and working musicians, worldwide, on
desktop and phone.

WHAT THE APP DOES: it takes a normal seven-note scale, removes one note, and
drills the resulting six-note scale in rhythmic groupings of 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7
notes that resolve exactly on the downbeat. It shows real staff notation and
plays real piano.

THE CENTRAL VISUAL METAPHOR — SUBTRACTION.
Six of something present, one absent. The absence should be visible and
deliberate, not an accident. Think: six dots and one hollow ring where the
seventh would be. Six lit keys and one dark. A ring of six with a gap. Whatever
you choose, the missing element must read as intentional and elegant, and it
should work as a favicon at 32x32.

DELIVER:
1. A wordmark for "SHADAVA" — set in Archivo, heavy. Consider incorporating the
   subtraction metaphor into the letterforms or as a small mark beside it. Also
   give a stacked lockup with the subtitle "six-note practice engine".
2. An app icon / favicon built from the metaphor, tested at 512px, 128px and 32px.
3. A landing-page hero, 1600x900, desktop. Dark. It must contain: the wordmark,
   one sentence of promise, one primary button reading "Start practising", and a
   visual of the six-note idea. Leave a clearly marked rectangular region where a
   live interactive element (a keyboard and a line of notation) will be dropped
   in by the developer — do not draw fake notation in it, just mark the space.
4. The same hero at 390x844 for mobile.
5. A colour and type specimen sheet showing the palette, the type scale, button
   states and how the "missing note" mark is used at each size.

COLOUR — dark-first, and it must be a music app that looks like a precision
instrument, not a toy:
   near-black bg  #0E0D0C
   surface        #1B1917
   deep red       #8B1E24   (the "removed" note — always red, always means absence)
   gold           #C9A227   (the active/current note, primary accent)
   cream          #F4EFE4   (primary text)
   muted          #8A8178   (secondary text)
Establish ONE rule and hold it everywhere: gold = the note you are playing,
red = the note that was removed, cream = the rest of the scale. That rule is the
entire information design of the app.

TYPE:
   Display / headings : Archivo
   Editorial accent   : Cormorant  (use sparingly, for the theory-teaching cards)
   Numerals, labels, code, tempo readouts : IBM Plex Mono

BRANDING: the real Nathaniel School of Music N mark plus the wordmark "Nathaniel
School of Music" must appear tastefully in the footer. Use the supplied logo PNG.

MOOD: precise, calm, scholarly, premium. The closest reference points are a
well-designed audio plugin and a beautifully typeset theory textbook. Not
gamified, no cartoon mascots, no gradient blobs, no glassmorphism.

DO NOT:
- Do not design the practice screen itself — only identity plus the landing hero.
- Do not draw staff notation; the app renders real notation and fake notation
  would have to be thrown away.
- Do not write "world's first" or any superlative.
- Do not use purple-to-pink AI-startup gradients.

DELIVER as PNG exports plus an editable Claude Design file.
```
