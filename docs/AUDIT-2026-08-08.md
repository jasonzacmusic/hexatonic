# Hexatonic audit — 8 August 2026

**Starting source:** `f7c6e49` on `origin/main`

**Live site checked:** `https://hexatonic.nathanielschool.com` (HTTP 200)

**Class source:** Music Gym — 3D — Hexatonic & Octatonic Scale Practice

## Baseline verdict

The latest main branch was healthy: type-check, all **238 tests**, and the
production build passed. The live site contained the newest cross-tradition
scale library from main. It did not yet contain the 8 August Movement Lab.

The theory engine was strong, but the chordal pedagogy was not exhaustive. The
app could enumerate chords and play Barry Harris movement, yet it could not run
the exact two-shape inversion exercise that made the class useful.

## Findings, ranked by musical impact

| Rank | Finding | Student impact | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Missing** | No six-note two-triad inversion ladder | Students saw chord lists but could not practise the class's central movement | Built in Movement Lab |
| **Missing** | Symmetric octatonic existed only as a scale | The app did not reveal its two interlocked diminished-seventh sets | Built in Movement Lab |
| **Wrong label** | Barry movement printed `B°7` on D-, F- and A-flat-bass voicings | It hid the inversion and made a root-position label factually misleading | Fixed as `B°7/D`, `B°7/F`, `B°7/Ab` |
| **Lukewarm** | Sixth/seventh equivalences were buried | The class's “two seventh-chord shapes” idea was hard to see | `C6 = Am7`, `Cm6 = Am7b5`, and diminished aliases are now explicit |
| **Naming risk** | The transcript called `1 2 3 4 5 6` the gospel/Sunday scale and blended Barry with the octatonic | Repeating this publicly would teach three different systems as one | Added an in-app naming repair and durable lesson bank |
| **Security** | Locked `nanoid 3.3.16` carried a high-severity advisory | No known exploit path in this UI, but the dependency gate was no longer clean | Updated lock to `3.3.18`; audit is clean |

## What was built

- A pure interlocked-movement theory engine.
- Hexatonic movement in 12 keys × 6 rotations.
- Symmetric-octatonic movement in 12 keys × 2 rotations.
- Block/arpeggio, up/up-down and tempo controls.
- Honest inversion names and every equivalent diminished-seventh reading.
- Six new movement tests, including exhaustive key/rotation sweeps and the
  browser-found case where a chord enters already inverted.
- Transcript-derived product and lesson decisions in
  `docs/10-MUSIC-GYM-MOVEMENT-LAB.md`.

## Deliberately not claimed complete

- The YouTube lesson and NSM public workshop are **not scripted or launched**.
  The bank contains a seed structure for Jason Zac to shape with the app.
- MIDI currently grades scalar notes, not full chord voicings, bass/inversion,
  timing or two-hand block-plus-arpeggio performance.
- There is no presenter-specific Movement Lab yet.
- No production deployment is part of this branch. The repository's audit rule
  requires a verified Preview first.
