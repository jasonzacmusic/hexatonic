Driving /practice

Six decisions, then play

  1. The scale. Key, Family, Mode / rotation. It opens on C minor hexatonic (Dorian/Aeolian), the one to start with. "Custom — build your own" lets you tap in any notes.
  2. The drill. Pattern: up, down, both, in thirds, in fourths, cells, janta, arpeggio ladder. Octaves 1–3. Top note adds the octave. Leave it off: six notes keeps the rhythm maths clean.
  3. The rhythm. Meter / tala, Subdivision (8ths, triplets, 16ths), Accent grouping (3–9), and Resolve (when it counts as landed).
  4. Read the banner. It tells you how many bars the drill takes to land back on beat 1, and how many seconds that is. If it says 15 bars, switch to triplets.
  5. Play. PLAY / STOP. − / + move the tempo 4 bpm at a time. Count-off, Loop and Click are toggles.
  6. Keep it. Copy drill link saves the exact setup as a link you can paste into WhatsApp. Print / PDF gives you the score on paper. With a MIDI keyboard in Chrome, the Play along panel grades your notes and accents.

Twelve stations

The same plan is at the bottom of /workout with tick boxes. They're saved on your device, so you can close the laptop between stations.

0:0015 min

Warm-up

/practice as it opens: C minor hexatonic, up and down, 84 bpm. Right hand, left hand, then both. Change Key to G, then D.

Done whenthree keys, hands together, no stops.

0:1530 min

Knock one out

/workout → Major. Before each row's ▶, sing the note that's about to vanish. Then Natural minor, Harmonic minor, Melodic minor. On two rows you like, press Study → Drill it in Practice →.

Done whenyou can say which rows are tritone-free without looking at the Tritones column.

0:4530 min

Blues and gospel

/improvise → the 12-bar blues tab. Improvise with the blues scale for three choruses, then the gospel (major blues) scale. On /workout → Blues, check Same six notes: C blues is E♭ major blues.

Done whenyou can move between the two sounds over one chorus without stopping.

1:1520 min

Symmetry: whole tone and augmented

/scales → Family Whole tone. The "same family in every key" grid shows only two distinct scales; play both. Then Augmented: four.

Done whenyou've played all six from memory.

1:3510 min

Break. Stand, water, no instrument.

1:4525 min

The fourths cycle

/practice → Pattern In fourths. Go round the keys: C G D A E B F♯ D♭ A♭ E♭ B♭ F. Start at 72 bpm, and add +4 after two clean passes.

Done whenall twelve keys are done. Say "perfect" out loud on each fourth.

2:1030 min

Rhythm: 3s, 4s, 5s, 7s

/live → presets Tisra, Chatusra, Khanda, Misra. Speak the konnakol while you play: ta-ki-ta, ta-ka-di-mi, ta-di-gi-na-thom, ta-ki-ta-ta-ka-di-mi. Then open /resolution to see why each one lands where it does.

Done whenyou land Khanda (5 bars) three times in a row.

Harmonisation

/workout → Major, no 4 → the Harmonisation card. For each note, play every chord chip, then play it yourself with the melody on top. Then /harmony → Triads and Pair atlas.

Done whenyou can harmonise C D E G A B top to bottom without the screen.

How to practise

  • Sing before you play. If you can't sing it, your fingers are guessing.
  • Tempo ladder. Start where it's easy, and add +4 bpm only after two clean passes.
  • Three speeds. /varisai locks ×1, ×2 and ×4 together, the traditional way to build speed.
  • Keys round the circle. Never practise only in C.
  • Keep the link. Copy the drill link of anything hard, and come back to exactly that tomorrow.

Good to know

  • Use Chrome or Edge. They're the only browsers that can hear a MIDI keyboard.
  • The first sound on each page takes a few seconds while the piano samples load. After that it's instant.
  • Switching tabs or pages stops the sound on purpose, so nothing keeps playing in the background.
  • On a phone, use "Add to Home Screen" and it opens like an app.
